## Part 1: Game Ideas
### Q5  Books / Movies  
#### Parasyte by Hitoshi Iwaaki  
The story is about a high school senior who is the victim of a failed attempt by a parasitic organism to take over his brain. The parasite, Migi, instead infects and takes over his arm, and both are forced in a peculiar partnership to fight other parasites.   

Based on the series can create a game that the player is a part of small town/community and need to find the person that is taken over by a parasite. The parasite can "infect" and control other people when they are interacting closely to it. person controlled by the parasite will have something unusual with his appearance. finding the first body the parasite controlled and kill him (maybe give the option to find a route to defeat the parasite without killing the person) will remove his control from all the people. during the search for it the controlled people will attack the player and he will need to choose if to find a way to restrain them or kill them, they won't come back to life after the parasite is gone. for the art style I would find a way for it to feels normal so the player will find it difficult to feel any differences caused by the parasite and very few environment that will be eerie.  

#### Sandman by Neil Gaiman  
The series of comics is about Dream, also known as Morpheus, who is the ruler of the Dreaming realm. The storyline explores his imprisonment, the consequences of his absence on the world, and his quest to regain his lost tools of power while interacting with various mythological and human characters  

Based on the series, we can a game about the first arc of the story, where Morpheus can escaped at the start and to retrieve his power and artifact he need to jump between dreams (this will diverge a little from the story) of different people as levels. In some dreams he will collects his artifacts, and some he will need to collect sand that will increase his power back. Every level can have a different design and short side story according to the person who have the dream, giving the developer a lot of freedom.  

#### 1984 by George Orwell  
Winston Smith is a member of the Outer Party, working at the Ministry of Truth in London, where he rewrites historical records to conform to the state's ever-changing version of history in a fictional future (book written in 1949) in the year 1984, Great Britain has become a province of the totalitarian superstate Oceania, which is led by Big Brother, a dictatorial leader supported by an intense cult of personality manufactured by the Party's Thought Police.  

Based on the story, we can create a stealth game, no fighting enemies directly. the player need to behave as a regular part of the cult and while trying to hurt them from within without being seen by anyone including the big brother. To decrease the power of the big brother he will need to sabotage electronics, learn new information, build new tools to help you in home when even inside it the big brother watvhes, every wrong step can lead to a bad ending (true to the ending of the book), the game will be monochromatic / dark to feel isolated, and during the progress of the game it will gain more colour.
